Events

Discover the exciting events that make KCC life unforgettable where students celebrate, compete, connect, and grow together as one family.

  1. Home
  2. Events
SANGYAW

Sangyaw is the official welcome of KCC to all its students especially to our freshmen and transferees. As our Acquaintance Party, it is a day dedicated to new beginnings, new friendships, and new memories as we open another school year together. Through fun games, exciting performances, music, and laughter, students get to know each other beyond the classroom. Sangyaw breaks the ice, builds confidence, and reminds every KCCian that they are not just classmates they are family. It is where smiles turn into friendships, and friendships turn into unforgettable memories.

HINUGYAW

Hinugyaw is more than just a competition it is a celebration of unity, talent, and school pride. As KCC's official Intramurals, students from different departments and year levels come together to compete in sports, dance, cheerdance, and cultural events. From the loud cheers of each team to the thrilling final games, Hinugyaw teaches teamwork, discipline, sportsmanship, and friendship where rivalries turn into respect, strangers become teammates, and every KCCian celebrates as one family.
